Frequently Asked Questions

Common information regarding birth details, consultation preparation and horoscope analysis.

What information is required for a consultation?

Your full name, date of birth, accurate time of birth, place of birth and the main topic or question you wish to discuss are required.

Why is an accurate birth time important?

The birth time determines the Ascendant, house positions and divisional charts. Even a small variation may affect some parts of the horoscope analysis.

Can a consultation guarantee a specific event?

No. Astrology is used to understand tendencies, timing patterns, opportunities and challenging periods. Responsible consultation should not guarantee an exact outcome.

How is the horoscope analysed?

Depending on the question, the analysis may consider the natal chart, planetary strength, yogas, divisional charts, Vimshottari Dasha and current transits.

Is the consultation conducted online?

Yes. Consultations may be conducted through a mutually agreed video or voice-call platform after the appointment is confirmed.

Are remedies suggested during the consultation?

Simple traditional or practical measures may be discussed where appropriate. Expensive, fear-based or guaranteed remedies are not promoted.
